# Script and audio brief — "The line across the margin"

Written for comparison runs. It is our own text, it is short enough to keep a
voice generation cheap, and it is deliberately plain: a model that reads it well
is doing the work, not benefiting from lucky punctuation.

## The narration

> The line across the margin started as a mistake. I left it in because it looked
> like the edge of a room. Now every page has a wall, and the wall has a window,
> and the window is where the light gets in.

Roughly 44 words, about 17 seconds at a calm pace.

## The brief

- **Voice.** Read it slowly, warm, close to the microphone. Pause after "mistake"
  and again after "room". Do not dramatise the last line; let it land flat.
- **Music.** One instrumental bed, 20 seconds, no percussion. Felted piano and a
  low sustained tone. It should sit under the voice without competing for the
  same frequencies.
- **Effects.** Two cues only: a single soft page turn at the start, and a distant
  room tone that fades out under the final word.

## Why this is safe to use

The words are written here, for this studio. There is no borrowed lyric, no
licensed sample and no real person's story. The reference still and the footage
in `comparison-fixtures.json` were made in this studio, so a comparison run needs
no rights clearance beyond our own decision to publish the result.

## How a comparison uses it

The same brief goes to every model in a job group, with the same length. When two
takes disagree, the disagreement is the models' work — not a different
instruction.
