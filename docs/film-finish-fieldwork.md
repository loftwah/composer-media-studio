# Film finishing fieldwork

[Studio #222](https://github.com/loftwah/composer-media-studio/issues/222) owns the finishing loop. The initial comparison uses the existing 30-second vertical teaser and its editable Remotion project. It reuses the public Low Tide artwork, the real Studio recording and the existing music bed. ## Actual 30-second comparison

The live original is 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, H.264 with 48 kHz stereo AAC. Its SHA-256 is `cdf4c9caecef30b490f0f028cd67fa1aa7e56f35314ce9fb189c630285ffe274`. At the start the artwork is dim inside the library. At 24.2 seconds the URL appears in captions while the visible “Build yours” action is still fading in.

An FFmpeg cut removed 24.0–25.8 seconds and made the action visible sooner. That local comparison ran 28.2 seconds. The editable Remotion revision kept 30.000 seconds, opens with the existing public Low Tide still, then shows the real library. The action and tutorial URL are fully visible at frame 735. The local render is 4,261,132 bytes with SHA-256 `0c6d72b61ab82171f6c2e5c599e644ef0ef6b4493f038fefea3024b9de7c637e`. Burned-in and sidecar captions use the same cue source. Both the original and the revision measured −16.47 LUFS and −4.33 dBTP. Full decode of the revision found no errors or black sections above the 0.3-second check threshold. Four sampled frames were inspected at 390 pixels.

The candidate is local. The published original and its source remain current. Human sound-on review and preference, a second independently styled project, and candidate publication approval remain open.
